Tint Tide Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Developer: Joey Studio

Tint Tide is a color-filling puzzle game. This page explains what data is used when you play and what choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect personal data and we do not run our own servers.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob; Google may collect some data as described below.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so we can fix the bug.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (such as which level you finished and in how many moves) are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can tune difficulty and ads.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, statistics and settings (sound, theme, language) are stored only on your device; we never receive this data. If you turn on device backup (iCloud or Google), it may be included in your own backup and restored when you reinstall. Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device. You can also erase your game progress in the app at Settings → Reset progress; your settings are kept. The only exception is the anonymous gameplay statistics described under "Gameplay statistics" below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interaction, diagnostic and performance data.

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some U.S. states), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • On iOS, you can deny or revoke tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact name varies by device).

If you decline, you can still play normally; any ads you see will not be personalized.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service. When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to Google containing:

  • the technical error details (stack trace) and the state of the app at that moment;
  • device model, operating system version and app version;
  • a random installation identifier generated by Firebase, which is not linked to you, your account or your advertising ID.

Crash reports contain no personal data, no game progress and no account information; we use them only to fix bugs. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Reports are kept for 90 days.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand where players get stuck and how often ads appear, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service. While you play, it sends:

  • game events: the level or mode you started or finished, whether you won, the number of moves and stars, whether you used undo or hints, and whether you skipped the tutorial;
  • ad events: when an ad was shown and when a reward video was not ready;
  • events the service records automatically, such as app opens, session length and app updates;
  • device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country (from your IP address);
  • a random app-instance identifier generated by Firebase and, where your device allows it, the advertising identifier.

We never send your name, email or any account information, and we use these statistics only in aggregate to improve the game. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. You can limit use of the advertising identifier in your device settings (see "Your choices"), and uninstalling the app stops all collection.

Children

Tint Tide is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
